# Third-party product artwork

Local copies of original product artwork retrieved on 2026-11-02. `sources.json` records each official source URL, discovery page and SHA-256 digest. Artwork retains its original proportions and colors; no runtime request is made to these sources.

The Codex icon is the artwork distributed by OpenAI's official VS Code extension. Its OpenAI blossom also identifies OpenAI, ChatGPT and Whisper. Apple artwork was converted from the official favicon's ICO container to PNG without redrawing it. Other files are unchanged copies from the listed official websites and brand kits.

Names and marks belong to their respective owners. They identify integrations and model makers; they do not imply endorsement. Follow the linked brand guidelines when reusing this artwork.
